# The library, line by line

What this is: every component and pattern the gallery draws, one line each — what the reviewers said, the Language entry it touches, and the choices their words leave open.
Where it comes from: `reviews/cf1.1-controls-fresh-eyes.md`, `reviews/cf1.1-signals-fresh-eyes.md`, `reviews/cf1.1-patterns-fresh-eyes.md`, `reviews/cf1.1-text-fresh-eyes.md`, `reviews/cf1.2-entries-fresh-eyes.md` (two replies), and the ten `reviews/ce3.2-<app>-fresh-eyes.md` dispositions.
Date: 2026-09-13.
How to rule: beside a line write keep / merge into X / drop / add / rename, or a word of your own.
Nothing here is a recommendation: the choices are listed in no order, and the drafter's opinion is not in this file.

## The controls

**Button** — Hover is the rest state repainted: rest `(0,122,255)` and hover `(0,122,255)`, identical in both schemes, while press matches the reference byte for byte. The disabled fill is byte-identical to the Tonal face (`236` light, `51,58,63` dark), so "a secondary action" and "unavailable" wear one face. — touches: Button, Emphasis, State — open: keep; drop Ghost, which "reads as static text with no fill and no edge"; rename the emphases to the platform's names; add the platform's borderless toolbar button — pool: 393, 405, 407

**Chip** — "The chip and the tonal button are one appearance at two heights": same fill `#ECECEC`, same press `#D5D5D5`, differing only by 20 px against 24 px and the radius. The four purposes are legible, but the entry's structure says a leading glyph where the Input chip leads with an avatar, and the same fill is drawn on three surfaces — 19 levels of separation on the content, 11 on a card or in the chrome. — touches: Chip, Emphasis, Fill, Level — open: keep; merge into Button; rename; add the platform's token — pool: none

**Text field** — The entry's three clauses are all drawn, and the render matches the platform's own field exactly, 28 px white with a 1 px `#F3F3F3` edge. The interior measures the content fill with no separation at all, told by a hairline alone, which the Level entry forbids for a raised thing; the measured edge does not read at 1x. — touches: Text field, Level, Seam — open: keep; rename to the platform's name — pool: 404, 405

**Search field** — "The search field is the text field with a magnifier glyph… byte-for-byte the same chrome", where the platform's is a filled capsule `#E8E8E8`, no stroke, ends fully rounded. The longest entry against one of the thinnest renders: the match count, which match is current, the stepping and the highlight itself are not drawn anywhere. — touches: Search field, Highlight, Text field — open: keep; merge into Text field; rename; add the platform's capsule — pool: 365, 408

**Checkbox** — Matches: unchecked, checked, focused, disabled, binary with no mixed state. One app draws it as a hard 16 px square where "the macOS checkbox is ~14×14 with a ~3.5px corner radius and a 1px stroke". — touches: Checkbox, Checked — open: keep — pool: none

**Radio** — It shares the checkbox render "and the sharing costs it": the entry's whole subject is a group whose options exclude each other, and what is drawn is four isolated specimens in states, with no group and therefore no exclusivity. — touches: Radio, Checkbox, Selection — open: keep — pool: none

**Switch** — No specimen in the gallery at all. It is first on the controls reviewer's missing list, measured in System Settings at 37×26 px, fill `#1576F2`, white knob: "Settings-shaped Mac UI is built out of switches." — touches: Switch, Checkbox — open: keep; add — pool: 357

**Picker** — "The best-served entry here": both triggers drawn, both variants legible, one value in the trigger, the current row marked. Its chevron is a solid grey triangle where the platform draws a stroked up-and-down pair; the trigger is 28 px where the platform's pop-up is 24, and carries a `#D5D5D5` stroke the platform's does not. — touches: Picker, Variant, Menu — open: keep; rename to pop-up, pull-down or combo box — pool: 329, 330, 416

**Menu** — Square-cornered, shadowless, with the highlight running edge to edge between the borders, `#0064E1`, corners hard; every selection in the captures is inset and rounded — System Settings' sidebar row is 18 px in with a ~7 px radius. "A square-cornered, shadowless, edge-to-edge-highlight menu is the one thing in the set that doesn't look like it came from this operating system." — touches: Menu, Level, Selection — open: keep; merge into Picker; rename — pool: 329, 416

**List** — The entry's four distinctions — choice, the selection state, menu-as-floating-list, table-as-list-with-columns — are none of them drawn: five plain rows, no selection, no hover, no separators, no columns. Rows are 36 px against Finder's 20 px pitch, with no alternating fill, no header, no glyphs. What is drawn and unnamed is virtualization. — touches: List, Table, Menu, Selection — open: keep; merge into Table; add the platform's source list — pool: 401

**Scrollbar** — "The flattest contradiction in the packet": the entry says the match marks lie beside the thumb and never under it, and thumb and marks occupy the identical six columns, x536–541. There is no track — no pixels in that column but thumb and marks. Across five reference windows, every one scrolled, "there is not a single visible scrollbar pixel". — touches: Scrollbar, Highlight, Scroll area — open: keep; drop the standing bar; add an overlay bar that fades — pool: 338, 339, 340, 342, 363, 389, 403

**Scroll area** — The edge dissolves over about 14 columns, `#FBFCFF` at x24 through `#6EA3FF` at x37, where "Finder's file list is cut dead at y=1020, mid-glyph, with one antialiasing row and then the window edge. No fade anywhere in any capture." Its horizontal bar lies over the content while the list render puts one in a gutter — two placements, one entry, no distinction. — touches: Scroll area, Scrollbar — open: keep; merge into Scrollbar; drop the edge dissolve — pool: none

**Pagination** — "Pagination duplicates scrolling. Nothing in any reference capture paginates; Finder shows 40+ rows in one scroll view." Only the current page has a face — the accent-filled button again — so the render does not support calling the other eight buttons, and previous and next are bare solid triangles with no disabled treatment. One app paginates too: "That is a web control." — touches: Pagination, Button — open: keep; drop — pool: none

**Breadcrumb** — "Breadcrumb overlaps Finder's path bar — same job, 'trail back to the root'" — but drawn as web links, `#0068DA` underlined with a filled triangle between segments, where "there is no coloured underlined text anywhere in the five reference windows" and Finder's path bar uses folder glyphs, grey chevrons and black labels. The entry says chevrons; the render draws triangles. — touches: Breadcrumb, Link, Icon — open: keep; drop; rename to the path bar — pool: none

**Paragraph** — "Paragraph is a text view, not a control… it doesn't belong in a control inventory beside checkbox and picker." The entry says "styled text" and stops where the render names five axes and three link states; its measured link hover shift "is what makes the button's and chip's flat hover read as an omission". — touches: Paragraph, Link, Measure — open: keep; merge into Markdown document; drop from the controls — pool: 349

## The signals

**Badge** — Sound where the entry is strict: Neutral wears the system grey, and the dismissible badges are the Info blue, not the theme colour. Wrong: three encodings of five statuses in one sheet, "fifteen objects for five meanings"; white on `#34C759` is 2.22:1 and on `#FF8D28` 2.31:1 against the worst white-on-colour in the references, 3.88:1; and the render draws "Filtered by owner ×", verbatim the example the entry rules out. — touches: Badge, Status, Signal, Contrast — open: keep; drop the disc rows; merge the disc into the pill; rename — pool: 371, 373, 400

**Alert** — "The four one-line alert rows and the four one-line toast rows are the same control twice": 520×56 with a coloured triangle against 240×35 with an 8 px bar, the same text colours, neither carrying a button. The status glyph is one solid right-pointing triangle recoloured four ways — "a play or disclosure affordance", so four stacked rows "read as a list of things to run". The entry's title-and-body pair is drawn nowhere. — touches: Alert, Status, Icon, Notification — open: keep; merge into Toast; rename to the platform's in-window banner; add a symbol per status — pool: 370, 394, 406

**Toast** — The entry describes four things that are not in the pixels: no hairline ("`255,255,255` at x275, then `247,247,247` at x276. No intermediate row"), no shadow, no glyph, and no close mark, "though the entry calls it its only control". It is lighter than the column it floats on in light and darker in dark. Against the pattern: "A stack of four toasts is Notification Center's job." — touches: Toast, Notifications, Notification, Level, Status — open: keep; merge into Alert; drop in favour of the platform's notifications; add the close and the action — pool: 332

**Tooltip** — "The closest match in the packet": the window fill, one separator hairline at the measured tenth, text only, no status, identical in both schemes, "every clause confirmed". One word is wrong — "beside its trigger", where the render puts it above and says so. Against it: "every NSView has a `toolTip` string and the system draws the help tag itself — delay, placement, shadow and all." — touches: Tooltip, Attachment, Level — open: keep; drop in favour of the platform's help tag; rename — pool: 332

**Icon** — Confirmed, including the part that looked wrong at 1x. But "the platform control marks are drawn at body-text weight": the disclosure, back and forward chevrons bottom out at `#272727` on white, 14.9:1, where the active Finder window's heaviest toolbar glyph is `#4D4D4D` (8.45:1) and its own back/forward pair `#BFBFBF` (1.84:1); they are 10×18 against Finder's 8×14. The info glyph is a 34×34 filled disc against the platform's 13×13 outline glyph. — touches: Icon, Signal, Mark — open: keep; drop the back/forward pair the toolbar already gives; add a weight of their own for the control marks — pool: 346

## The patterns

**Accordion** — The entry names a chevron turned by open state; the render draws solid deltas. The hairlines it never mentions are placed so the rule reads as "under the header", and the closed section headed *How a section is bounded* "is, in this render, not bounded". The open body reserves about 100 px it does not use — "a one-line section leaves a hole the size of two more sections". — touches: Accordion, Seam — open: keep; rename the glyph — pool: 413

**Card** — Fill measured exactly as written: `247` on `255`, `42,48,52` on `30`, no hairline, no shadow. But the dark separation is also cooler, and that has no word — it is what tells a card from a table stripe. The entry puts the developer's badge in the header; the render puts it at the foot. In light the card is darker than the content, where System Settings' grouped box is lighter than its page. — touches: Card, Level, Fill, Badge — open: keep; merge into Group — pool: 372, 425

**Group** — "The most accurate entry here": hairline `230` on `255` and `52` on `30`, exactly what the Seam entry defines, nothing inside, "literally true in the pixels". Against its neighbours: "Card, Group and the recommended pricing tier are one control… one box with a fill switch, presented as four patterns." — touches: Group, Card, Seam — open: keep; merge into Card; rename to the platform's grouped box — pool: none

**Hero** — The entry's optional visual is simply absent, which "optional" covers. What it does not claim is the pairing the render commits to — an accent face beside a tonal one, "the only place in the set where two emphases appear side by side". The headline measures 34 px where the largest type in any capture is 23 px, "and that is a full-window placeholder". — touches: Hero, Eyebrow, Emphasis, Typography role — open: keep; drop; move out of the native set — pool: none

**Feature** — "The render draws no icons. It draws three plain filled discs, each a different hue" — where the Language says a glyph "carr[ies] no status and no role of its own… the developer does not choose a colour for it". And "three different hues are the one thing that stops many features reading as one set." — touches: Feature, Icon, Signal — open: keep; drop; move out of the native set — pool: none

**Pricing** — The entry says tiers laid side by side as cards; measured, the outer two are groups (hairline, no fill) and only the middle is a card, which is what the Group entry's own guidance says. The entry's word for the middle one is a button word doing a level's work. All three tier buttons carry the accent fill: "three equally-weighted primary actions in one row is exactly what the Emphasis entry exists to prevent". — touches: Pricing, Card, Group, Emphasis, Badge — open: keep; rewrite as groups with the recommended one a card; drop; move out of the native set — pool: none

**Testimonial** — "One centred card or a row of them": the render draws one card at x=48 in a 900-wide frame, hard left, with 340 px of empty page beside it. "It is not centred in either scheme. The entry offers two shapes and the render is neither." Its portrait slot and second attribution line are unnamed; in one app the monograms are a hairline circle with a grey letter, 2.19:1, "where the platform fills the circle and sets the monogram white". — touches: Testimonial, Card — open: keep; drop; move out of the native set — pool: none

**Modal** — The entry describes one shape and the render draws two, "and neither is that shape"; the rule the render is actually built around — one that demands an answer has no close, one that only reports has no footer — is missing from the words. "A document-scoped decision on macOS is a sheet: it slides down from the title bar, stays attached to the window, and dims nothing." The scrim is black at 0.196 in light and 0.367 in dark, with no note. — touches: Modal, Scrim, Level — open: keep; rename to the sheet; add the sheet as its own thing — pool: 321, 331, 332

**Popover** — "Floating beside its anchor" and the render places it below; it holds two lines of prose and nothing operable, which is "the entry's whole reason-for-being over a tooltip". Light tells it by a shadow with no outline (deepest value 234, a 1.19:1 separation); dark by a hairline with almost no shadow. — touches: Popover, Attachment, Level, Tooltip — open: keep; merge into Menu; rename — pool: 332

**Navbar** — "Navbar and Tabs are the same control drawn twice, badly": one horizontal row of words with a blue underline under the selected one, the same blue in both, differing only in that one underline is 2 px and the other 1 px — "two patterns, one idea, and they disagree on a detail nobody chose". What is drawn trailing is a version badge where the entry promises actions. — touches: Navbar, Tabs, Chrome, Badge, Toolbar — open: keep; merge into Tabs; drop; rename to the toolbar — pool: none

**Tabs** — "macOS has no underlined tab. It has the segmented control — a capsule of joined segments with the selected one filled. An underlined word row is Material Design and web nav, and it appears here twice." The strip also wears the chrome material at component scale, which the Chrome entry rules out in as many words. Three separate applications filed the same complaint. — touches: Tabs, Chrome, Navbar, Selection — open: keep; rename to the segmented control; merge into Navbar; drop the underline — pool: none — **also pool 586: two single-symbol chrome controls stand apart where the platform groups them as one segmented control; the same missing component**

**Sidebar** — The pill is exact against Voice Memos — `(23,139,251)` light, `(25,148,252)` dark — "including the fact that it gets lighter in dark mode the way the platform's does". Against that: the selected row's glyph is the same blue as its own pill, "contrast against its own pill is essentially 1:1"; the row glyphs are discs in four chosen hues where Finder's sidebar has zero saturated pixels; the pill is the full row height, so two adjacent selected rows would touch; and the collapsed rail "is a second sidebar that says less". — touches: Sidebar, Selection, Icon, List — open: keep; drop the collapsed rail; add section headers and counts — pool: 411, 412

**Pane** — "This entry disagrees with its render more sharply than any other." The surround measures `161` light and `40` dark where the window fill is `255`/`30` everywhere else — "off in opposite directions per scheme" — the document is set directly on it at 5.78:1 where the content fill would give about 13:1, and a full separator is drawn all the way round, which the entry explicitly rules out. "The pane is read overwhelmingly through its lightness in light… and the polarity flips between schemes." — touches: Pane, Backdrop, Seam, Contrast — open: keep; drop; merge into Group — pool: 351

**Shell** — The entry names three regions; the render has five, with the aside and the footer unnamed. Structurally: "the Mac window is columns that run top to bottom", the sidebar material continuing up into the toolbar band, where the shell lays a full-width band across the top and starts its columns below it — "That is a web page header, and it is the one arrangement AppKit does not make." Its content-to-aside seam is 6 px at full separator strength where every other seam in the same image is 1 px. — touches: Shell, Chrome, Seam, Inspector, Status bar, Toolbar — open: keep; rename; add the title bar and the toolbar band — pool: 366

**Splitter** — The three goldens hold the line at 1 px in all three, "so 'at the seam's width' holds here". The line falsifies the other half of the rule: over `(34,85,136)`, black at a tenth would be `(31,77,122)` and the measured line is `(230,230,230)` — "the splitter is painting the light-scheme result of the rule instead of applying the rule". There is no dark golden, so half the rule is untested. — touches: Splitter, Seam, Scheme — open: keep; rename — pool: none

**Table** — "Two different tables that share almost nothing": the section render has no cell rules, a plain header and alternating rows; the markdown goldens rule every cell, band the header and align per column, with no alternation and no sort affordance. The alternating band stops at x≈425 where Finder's runs the full width, and the sort glyph is a solid triangle where Finder's is a thin chevron. — touches: Table, List, Chrome — open: keep; merge the two; add column headers, sort and selection — pool: 401

**Notifications** — The column, the stacking and the anchor are all drawn, and Warning measures 28°, "plainly orange, exactly as Highlight claims". But Info measures 206–208°, which is also the accent: "For three of the four it does. For Info the hue also means 'this is the primary action' and 'this row is selected', so it indicates nothing." The status rides on a single 8 px leading bar and nothing else, and no dismiss is drawn. — touches: Notifications, Notification, Toast, Status, Accent — open: keep; drop in favour of the platform's notifications; add a dismiss — pool: none

## The text surfaces

**Markdown document** — The closing clause "everything else is read" against a render that draws task checkboxes — "whatever their behaviour, they wear a control's whole appearance" — a horizontal scrollbar, a blockquote bar, a rule, a table, inline code chips and four heading levels whose top two are nearly the same size; the images the entry names are not drawn. Body text is `(0,0,0)` on white, 21.0:1, where Mail's is `(39,39,39)` at 14.9:1. — touches: Markdown document, Paragraph, Table, Image, Highlight — open: keep; add entries for what it draws; rename the inline code chip — pool: 427

**Code block** — No entry, "and the render makes the case for one": the fence is `(239,241,245)` light and `(30,30,46)` dark — the same lightness as the page, told apart by 16 levels of blue alone. Its syntax palette is "the largest field of developer-chosen colour in the entire set", and its comments are "the least legible text in the block" at 2.30:1 light and 3.36:1 dark, while being the longest prose in it. — touches: Markdown document, Fill, Signal, Contrast (no entry of its own) — open: keep; add an entry; rename — pool: 352, 369

**Highlight** — "The best-served entry in the set, and the one with the sharpest single defect." In the content the current match really is stronger and yellow is the highlight's alone. On the scrollbar it is not: the ordinary mark reads 1.06:1 in light and the current one 1.11:1, "a difference of five hundredths, unreadable", and the same mark reads 4.9:1 over the thumb against 1.06:1 on the bare track, so legibility depends on scroll position. Marks dimmed below the current one are an invention: "Mail does not pale down its non-current hits at all." — touches: Highlight, Scrollbar, Contrast — open: keep; drop the scrollbar marks; add a track — pool: 336, 337, 338, 343, 344, 362, 403

**Link** — Colour and underline are drawn as written, "showing its affordance in the text itself". Hover moves `(0,104,218)` to `(0,99,207)` in light, "below any threshold at which a reader would notice a state change", and focus draws a rounded ring — 200 measured pixels of it — "a clearly-drawn, deliberate affordance with no word anywhere in the Language". Elsewhere: "there is no coloured underlined text anywhere in the five reference windows." — touches: Link, Focused, Highlight — open: keep; rename; add a word for the ring — pool: 349, 350

**Heading word** — "The goldens cannot settle the rule, and that is itself the finding." The heading word and an ordinary link are byte-identical — `(0,104,218)` light, `(65,156,255)` dark, both underlined — so "a reader cannot tell them apart", and a still cannot say whether the capture is at rest or with the key held. The carve-outs hold; `SETUP` is the one case where the two readings disagree. — touches: Link, Highlight (no entry of its own) — open: keep; add an entry; add a render that shows the held key — pool: 350

## Across the set

**Hover** — "This is the biggest one and it's exact": rest and hover are pixel-identical on the button and on the chip, in both schemes, while the platform's hover is a real fill appearing — `#FFFFFF` to `#F2F2F2` in light, `#384146` in dark, in exactly one region of the capture. Press, by contrast, matches the system byte for byte: "the press token was derived from the real system; the hover token was not applied." The link and the badge's close mark do move, "so this is not 'a still capture shows no cursor'". — touches: State, Button, Chip, Link, Fill — open: keep; add the platform's hover fill wherever a control is operated — pool: 371, 389, 407 — **settled by measurement, no ruling needed: the platform's hover fill lands in CG5.2**

**The toolbar** — "Not one of the sixteen patterns is a toolbar, and every single real window is organised around one", and the sidebar material continues up into it: `(247,247,247)` sampled at x=200 over the sidebar against `(255,255,255)` at x=700 over the content. Four of the ten applications were filed for having none. — touches: Toolbar, Chrome, Shell, Navbar — open: add — pool: 418, 428

**The levels** — In light, chrome and the raised fill are the same byte, `247,247,247`, so "a card and a sidebar are not told apart by fill at all in light"; the five named levels resolve to two distinct fills in light and three in dark, and no render in either packet contains a backdrop. The Level entry says no level is lighter or darker than another by rule and the Chrome entry says chrome is darker than the content in both: "the renders back Chrome and falsify Level." In light a table's alternating row steps further from the page than a card does. — touches: Level, Backdrop, Chrome, Card, Surface, Elevation — open: keep; rename; drop the numbering the Toast and Tooltip entries cite — pool: 372, 425

**The window's outline** — One application paints a `(161,161,161)` hairline around its own rounded corners: "Neither `notes-window.png` nor `textedit-window.png` has an inner frame line — the system draws the frame and shadow." Same cause as the pane's surround and the rail's hairline. — touches: Pane, Seam, Backdrop — open: keep; drop — pool: 351

**The scrollbars** — Across five reference windows, every one of them scrolled, "there is not a single visible scrollbar pixel": the platform's scrollers are overlay and absent from a still. One application draws two of its own that disagree with each other — 4 px at grey 122 and 6 px at grey 106 in the same window — and a code fence keeps a third, permanent and horizontal. The standing bar also sits about 360 px from the right edge of its panel, "not against the content it measures". — touches: Scrollbar, Scroll area — open: keep; drop the standing bar; add the overlay bar that fades — pool: 339, 342, 358, 368, 389, 390
